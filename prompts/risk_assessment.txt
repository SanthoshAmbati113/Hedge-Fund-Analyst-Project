- market signals

- news narrative

and produce a structured risk assessment.

{fundamentals}

{news_narrative}

--------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------

- volatility
- trend direction
- momentum

2. FUNDAMENTAL RISK
- financial health
- profitability

3. NARRATIVE RISK
- sentiment direction

--------------------------------------------------

- Strong fundamentals + negative sentiment → narrative risk

- volatility
- trend instability
- price movement

--------------------------------------------------

BEAR SCENARIO:
- what goes wrong
- probability

CONFIDENCE RULE
--------------------------------------------------

- data missing
- conflicting signals
- weak narrative clarity

- key_risks (minimum 3)
