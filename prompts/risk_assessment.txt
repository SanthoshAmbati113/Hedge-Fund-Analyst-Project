High volatility = higher uncertainty/risk regardless of direction — never automatically bearish.

3. NARRATIVE RISK: news sentiment, narrative direction, dominant themes, major catalysts, reported risks, uncertainty, consistency of news flow. Distinguish positive catalysts (support current position) from negative catalysts (create financial/operational/regulatory/strategic risk). Positive sentiment does NOT automatically eliminate fundamental/market risk.

RISK PRIORITIZATION: Prioritize risks that (1) are supported by multiple sources, (2) have high potential impact, (3) are persistent not isolated, (4) arise from cross-source conflicts, (5) could materially change the risk profile. KEY DRIVERS: ≥3 factors (positive, negative, or neutral) currently shaping the risk profile. Not predictions — current factors only.
